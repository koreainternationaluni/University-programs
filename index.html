Key Benefits

1. Practical Training & Income Opportunity

From the second semester of the first year, students may participate in standard field training after passing the company interview. Monthly income is stated as ₩2.8 million to over ₩3.5 million, based on 6 days per week and 10 hours per day.

🎓

2. Reduced Academic Burden

There are no midterm/final exams or video lectures. Approved company work is recognized as attendance and can be counted toward academic credit.

🏫

3. School Attendance During the First Semester

During the first semester, students attend approximately 1–2 days per week. Part-time job assistance may also be available during the first semester.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if the university is far from where I live?

After the first semester, students can be assigned to a company in a preferred area for work.

Can I change from D-4 to D-2?

Current D-4 language students may be eligible to change to D-2 if they meet the requirements. Final visa issuance is subject to immigration review and approval.

How much are tuition and dormitory fees?

Tuition is approximately ₩1.4M–₩1.9M, and dormitory fees are approximately ₩0.9M–₩1.2M per semester.

Eligibility (D-4 Students)

  • ✔️ TOPIK Level 2 or higher, or KIIP Level 2 or higher
  • ✔️ English track: IELTS 5.5 or higher
  • ✔️ Students who have completed 6 months or more of language study may be eligible even without a certificate.

* Field training from the second semester is subject to passing the company interview.
